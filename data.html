Data

The data behind this site come from many agencies, each counting something slightly different, on its own calendar and with its own gaps. None of it is a complete record of who arrives and who leaves. The Census estimates net migration and revises it with each new release, and two of its years cover only three months. Visas record permission to travel, not arrival. ICE removals here are only those that began with an ICE arrest recorded since October 2022, counted by calendar year of departure, so 2022 covers October to December only. Border Patrol removals are not published by destination country, so where they go is estimated. Detention figures are averages, not counts of individual people. We have tried to show each source as published, to label estimates and partial years, and to avoid reading more into the numbers than they hold.
